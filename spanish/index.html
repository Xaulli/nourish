Your gentle plan

A little and often beats a giant study session once a month.

🌱 Day 1 · LearnDo 5–8 flashcards. Say each Spanish word aloud.
🌿 Day 2 · RevisitRepeat yesterday's cards, then learn a few new ones.
🌼 Day 3 · ListenWatch 5–10 minutes of beginner Spanish video. Notice familiar words.
🌳 Day 4 · Use itPractise three useful phrases as if you were ordering or asking for help.
🍊 Day 5 · Check inTake the quick quiz. Review anything that felt tricky.
